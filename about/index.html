About

Two decades inside high-tech companies

Innovatory Consulting was founded to give technology companies one experienced partner for the technical and commercial work around their products.

Our story

For more than 20 years we have worked with companies that design complex technology: writing and reviewing the documents engineers rely on, running the marketing programs and trade shows that bring in customers, and building the websites, databases and software that hold it together.

Over that time the tools changed, but the problem did not. Great products still lose to competitors that explain themselves better. Today that also means using AI well, which is why we build local LLMs, RAG systems, AI tutors and chat assistants for clients.

We also build our own products. Through Salty Yak Apps we design and publish education and consumer apps, which keeps our software skills current and our advice practical.

Our mission

Help technology companies explain, market and support their products accurately and efficiently, with the right mix of expertise and software.

  • Accuracy first. Technical content is checked against the product, not just proofread.
  • Senior people. You work directly with experienced practitioners.
  • Practical technology. We recommend AI and software only where it pays off.
  • Respect for data. Private by default, in client work and in our own apps.
