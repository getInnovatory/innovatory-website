Technical content

Application notes, data sheets and white papers

Technical documents are often the first thing a design engineer reads about your product. We write new ones and review existing ones so they are accurate, consistent and easy to act on.

  • New application notes, data sheets, white papers and product briefs
  • Technical review: accuracy, completeness and consistency with the product
  • Editing for clarity, structure and a consistent house style
  • Diagrams, tables and specifications that engineers can use directly
  • Working directly with your engineering team to get the details right
AI solutions

Local LLMs, RAG systems, AI tutors and chat assistants

We design, build and support AI systems that solve a specific problem. Where data is sensitive, models run locally on your own hardware. Where accuracy matters, answers come from your own documents, with sources.

We have built each of these system types ourselves, end to end.

  • Local LLMs: open-weight models selected, tuned and deployed on your servers or private cloud, so confidential data never goes to a third-party API
  • RAG systems: retrieval-augmented generation over data sheets, manuals, support tickets and internal knowledge bases, with citations back to the source
  • AI tutors: guided learning assistants for product training, onboarding and education apps that explain, quiz and adapt to the learner
  • Website chat assistants: a chat assistant on your site that answers from your own content and hands off to a person when it should
  • AI strategy and support: choosing where AI helps and where it does not, evaluating quality, and keeping systems maintained
